01
Activation

Onboarding & the free trial

From install to a configured, ready-to-trade business in minutes. Every step strips out friction while the trial removes the risk of trying, the sequence that decides whether a download becomes an account.

02
Retention

The daily dashboard

The screens an instructor opens every day. Daily utility is what turns a trial into a habit, and a habit into a renewal.

03
Foundation

Learners

One record per learner replaces the paper diary, the notes app and the spreadsheet. Clean learner data is the foundation every other feature stands on.

04
Core revenue

Booking lessons

Taking a booking is the core revenue action. NexDrive makes it a few taps, prices it automatically, and sends the learner a branded confirmation, the difference between a lesson taught and a lesson paid for.

05
Daily operations

Calendar, lessons & navigation

Running the teaching day, seeing the week, working the day, managing each lesson and getting to the pickup, without ever leaving the app.

06
Growth driver

Refer, reward & credit

The growth loop. Instructors refer instructors, rewards accrue as credit, and credit comes off the next bill, acquisition and retention funded by the product itself.

07
Monetisation

Subscription & billing

Every plan is self-serve. Longer terms raise lifetime value; credit and upgrades keep the relationship extending, without a single support call.

08
Trust & resilience

Edge cases & resilience

Where the product protects the instructor from themselves, clashes caught, bad data blocked, and first-run states that drive activation rather than bewilder.
