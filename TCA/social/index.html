Before publishing: replace each placeholder block with commissioned photography of real members, rewrite the dummy copy against the pillar named in the badge, and write alt text for every image — never left blank. If a photo needs tying to the brand, use a single flat tint of Royal Blue or Ink at 15–25% across the whole frame, not a duotone.

One deliberate deviation: pillar badges are set at 16px rather than the 11–12px web token. At export scale these assets are viewed at roughly half size in-feed, where 12px stops being legible and the badge fails the Accessibility pillar it belongs to.
