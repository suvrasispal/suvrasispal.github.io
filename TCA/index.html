About us

Health inequality is the injustice nobody talks about.

The ethos of the Confidence Academy is very simple: participants get their money back once they attend their selected fitness and wellbeing events.

When you show up for your health, when you show up for yourself… your refund is your reward. Rayhana Sultan · Founder & Director

The Confidence Academy exists to make the tools of confidence-building — through improved nutrition, better mental and physical fitness, and community support — accessible to all, particularly to struggling and marginalised communities who often feel invisible or left behind. Health inequality is one of the biggest injustices of them all that we don't talk about, yet our health is the sole foundation of our existence.

The inception of this CIC is driven by the fact that nearly 60% of the UK population in 2023/24 were reported as obese or overweight. This is a national crisis in a context where our healthcare system is overstretched, support for mental health is not on par — which can be exacerbated by health-related problems — and an ageing population is awaiting us. Growing old, or not being able to afford the regular gym, should not mean fitness services are inaccessible to you. So we are here to change it.

Benefits

Good for your body. Better for your head. Best with other people.

Our sessions are built around Zumba and dance fitness — movement that feels like a night out rather than a workout. Here is what you actually get out of it.

Mental health & confidence

Confidence is not something you either have or you don't. It is built, one session at a time, usually while laughing.

  • Confidence that carries beyond the room
  • A real lift in mood
  • Stress relief and headspace
  • Room to express yourself, badly or brilliantly
  • Motivation that sticks, and feeling good about yourself

Community & social

Most people arrive on their own. Almost nobody leaves feeling like they came on their own.

  • Meet new people from your area
  • Build real friendships
  • Feel part of something local
  • Exercise together instead of alone
  • A welcoming, supportive room — always

Deposit-refund model

Your money comes back when you do.

This is the part people ask about most, so here it is in plain English. You are not paying for a class. You are leaving a deposit, and collecting it when you attend.

Why we use a deposit

  • Commitment. A booking you have skin in is a booking you keep.
  • Accessibility. Nobody is priced out by a monthly membership.
  • Reliable attendance. Instructors and venues can plan properly.
  • Sustainability. Halls, insurance and music licensing all have to be paid for.
  • Viability. Empty rooms are how community sessions quietly disappear.
  • Fairness. A no-show takes a place someone else wanted.

How it works

Four steps. That is the whole thing.

  1. Pay your deposit

    Book your place with a card tap or online payment. Deposits currently range from £10 to £45 depending on the session.

  3. Attend your session

    Turn up, move, meet people, enjoy it. We mark you as attended on the day.

  5. Your deposit is returned

    We send it back by bank transfer to the account details you give us. No forms, no chasing, no vouchers.

  7. Back with you in 24 hours

    Refunds are issued within 24 hours of the session you attended.

What happens if you don't attend?

If you don't attend the session, your deposit is kept — no exceptions. We know that sounds firm, and it is meant to. It is the single rule that keeps the whole model working: it is why we can hold places open, book venues in advance, and keep sessions affordable for everyone else in the room.

How refunds are issued

Your deposit is refunded by bank transfer within 24 hours of attending the session. We use the bank details you provide at booking, and you'll get written confirmation from us. We don't refund in cash, and we don't issue credit or vouchers.

Frequently asked questions about the deposit

How much is the deposit?

Deposits range from £10 to £45 depending on the session. The exact amount is always shown before you pay — you will never be charged an amount you haven't seen first.

How do I pay, and how do I get it back?

At community stalls and events we take deposits by card tap through SumUp. Refunds are issued separately, by bank transfer, within 24 hours of you attending.

Always ask for a printed receipt or email confirmation at the point of payment. You should get one automatically — if you don't, chase us.

Do I get the full amount back?

The working model in TCA's internal planning is a £10 deposit taken at a stall, with £8 (80%) returned after attendance and £2 retained to cover transaction and administration costs. That does not match "your full deposit back", so this wording needs a decision before this page goes live.

TCA to confirm: is the refund the full deposit, or the deposit minus a stated admin retention? Whichever it is, the exact figure should be stated here and at the point of payment.

What if I'm ill and can't make it?

Policy to be confirmed

The current model states plainly that a deposit is kept if you do not attend, with no exceptions. No separate illness policy has been set yet.

TCA to confirm: whether there is any provision for illness — for example transferring a deposit to a later session with advance notice — or whether the no-exceptions rule applies in every case. We will not publish an exception that hasn't been agreed.

What if TCA cancels the session?

Policy to be confirmed

If a session is cancelled by us, no reasonable model would keep your deposit — but the exact commitment hasn't been documented in the source materials yet.

TCA to confirm: the refund timeframe and method when TCA cancels, and whether attendees are automatically moved to the next available session or refunded by default.

Why should I trust handing money to a new organisation?

Fair question, and one we'd rather you asked than swallowed. The Confidence Academy is a registered Community Interest Company. At any stall or session you should see TCA branding, our CIC registration number, and volunteers carrying written authority from us.

Always take a printed receipt or email confirmation. If any of those things are missing, don't hand over money — contact us directly first.

Is this a membership or a subscription?

Neither. There is no recurring payment, no minimum term and nothing to cancel. You book the sessions you want, one at a time.
